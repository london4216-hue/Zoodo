# Fable Wednesday review handoff

ZOO is a throughput-focused automation proving ground, not the production governance system. Builders execute BUILD/FIX/PR work throughout the week without waiting for Fable. Record each completed attempt with agent ID, ticket/PR, timestamps, branch/commit, merge/deploy status, self-declared result, and any replacement handoff. Do not claim completion without the corresponding event; missing evidence is a reporting gap, not a reason to halt unrelated tickets.

Every Wednesday Fable ingests these records plus the **current Transcend governance rules and scorecard from their authoritative repository** (do not fabricate or silently replace those rules). Fable evaluates throughput, rework, risk, agent effectiveness, portability and implications for Transcend. Friday planning may update ZOO architecture. Fable does **not** impose Transcend risk scoring on BeautyPath or use BeautyPath as a governance gate. Keep ZOO experimental output separate from Transcend production release decisions.

Required scorecard fields: week, agent, builds, fixes, PRs merged, elapsed work time, rework, failed handoffs, replacement recruits, deploys, portability/risk notes. See #75.
